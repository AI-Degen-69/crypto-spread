Update #381 — went live

One rule for “which market are we trading right now?”

Before: the trading screen and the data recorder each carried their own copy of that question — and the copies disagreed on the edges.

Now: both ask the same single helper, so they always answer identically — and the answer is locked in by 25 automatic checks.

What changed in plain terms

Before

  • Two copies of the same “pick the live market” logic, one per corner of the system.
  • A fix applied to one copy silently missed the other.
  • Broken or malformed market rows could slip through and break a poll.
  • At the exact second a market ends, behaviour was undefined.

Now

  • One helper answers the question for everyone.
  • Any broken row is quietly skipped instead of breaking the run.
  • A market counts as open from its start second up to, but not including, its end second.
  • 25 new checks compare the helper against the old behaviour, edge case by edge case.

The rule, written out once

Rule 1

Scan every market across every listed event.

Rule 2

Skip anything missing its pair of trading tokens or its start/end times.

Rule 3

Keep only markets whose window contains the current moment.

Rule 4

If several are open, the one that started most recently wins.

Rule 5

If two started in the same second, the first one listed is kept.

Rule 6

If nothing is open, the answer is simply “no live market right now”.

Same answer for the trading screen and the data recorder — every time, at every minute boundary.
