Microstructure Anatomy: Why Baseline Bleeds -$165.66
The Single-Leg Decay Trap
On Polymarket 5m/15m UP/DOWN markets, binary tokens expire at either $1.00 or $0.00. When placing bids 2¢ from mid without leg chasing:

1. An aggressive market order sweeps your UP bid at 52¢ during an upward breakout.
2. The market surges to 85¢. The opposite DOWN bid at 48¢ never fills.
3. If the trend reverses or stalls, or if you held DOWN instead, you expire holding a single leg worth $0.00.
4. Result: -$52.00 loss per 100 shares. Every completed pair makes only $4.00 profit, so just one orphan leg wipes out 13 successful round trips!
Mechanic 1: Spread Offset as Adverse Selection Shield

How offset distance controls fill toxicity

At Offset = 0.02, you are first in line to be executed by informed taker flow. Widening offset to 0.035 - 0.080 for BTC/ETH and 0.150 - 0.200 for alts forces informed traders to cross a massive liquidity threshold. Only noise oscillations trigger fills, leaving both bids safe to complete the pair.
Mechanic 2: Leg Chase Converts Orphans to Hedged Pairs

Dynamic hedging when one leg gets hit

When enable_leg_chase=True, the moment Leg A fills, the engine actively reprices Leg B upwards towards the current ask (up to max_pair_cost, e.g. $0.98). Even if pair margin drops from 4¢ to 1¢, a completed pair guarantees a $1.00 settlement payout on expiration, turning a -$50 disaster into a +$1 locked-in gain.
