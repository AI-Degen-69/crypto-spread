Does testing ideas slow down the live bot? Now we have a number.

Problem: the live bot trades real money while you test ideas on old data at the same time. Nobody knew if the testing steals power from the live bot. Fix: we put a stopwatch on the bot's heartbeat and measured quiet vs. busy. Result: usually fine, sometimes a 3-second freeze.

The three numbers that matter

Heartbeat = the bot checking the market, about once a second. Measured while idle, then while a full test of 164,260 past moments ran beside it.

+6 ms
Typical heartbeat delay. Heartbeat stays ~1 second. You would never feel this.
+584 ms
Bad moments run ~57% late (1.0s becomes 1.6s). Happens in bursts.
3.3 s
Longest single freeze measured under load. Idle never passed 1.03 seconds.

Verdict: worth fixing, but no rush to rebuild

Think of it like one kitchen shared by two cooks. The live cook works every second. The testing cook suddenly chops 164,260 vegetables non-stop. The live cook usually still gets the stove, but sometimes waits 3 seconds. For 5-minute trades, a 3-second wait at the wrong moment can change the price you get.

Quiet kitchen

  • Heartbeat steady at ~1 second
  • Longest gap ever: 1.03 seconds

Testing cook working beside

  • Typical beat still ~1 second (+6 ms)
  • Bursts up to 1.6 seconds, worst 3.3 seconds

Next options on the table: give testing its own kitchen (separate process), teach it to pause for breath every 1,000 items, or allow only one test at a time. Decision comes after this number — nothing was rebuilt yet.
