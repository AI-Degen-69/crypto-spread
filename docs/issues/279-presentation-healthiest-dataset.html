Change put live · #279

Backtests now open on your healthiest data

Before, every test run opened on “everything mixed together” — good days and broken days alike. Now the screen checks each saved day, puts a star on the healthiest one, and opens on it. Your own choice still always wins.

Touch it: tap a day to change its health

Tap a card to cycle it between healthy, partial, and failed — watch the star move exactly the way the real screen moves it.

What the check looks at

  • Full capture
    Was the whole day recorded, with no missing stretch?
  • Clean bill
    Did the recording pass its integrity check?
  • Readiness first
    Research-grade days beat exploring ones; ties break on more clean slices, then the newest day.

See it in the project

  1. Open the dashboard and go to the Backtest tab.
  2. Look at the file list: one row carries a star that names why it won.
  3. Look at the dataset menu: it opens on the starred day. Pick anything yourself — including “everything” — and your pick sticks.
