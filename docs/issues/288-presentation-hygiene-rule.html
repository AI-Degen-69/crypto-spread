One small rule keeps the shared workspace clean

Every finished job used to leave clutter behind — orphan pages, half-deleted plans — until someone tidied up by hand. Now the cleanup rule is written down, so the mess stops coming back.

The shared project folder. Press the switch.

The rule, in three lines

  1. Finished pages get filed the same day.
    Whoever creates an explanation page saves it with the job — never left lying around.
  2. Scrap paper goes out after the job ships.
    The closing sweep deletes drafts of finished jobs — never left half-torn.
  3. Look at the desk before you push.
    If anything shows up that is not your own work, stop and ask.

See it in the project

  1. Where to look: open docs/git-workflow.md and scroll to section 7 at the bottom.
  2. What you will see: rules for filing issue artifacts, removing per-issue scratch files after merge, and checking before push that git status --porcelain shows only the issue's own work.
