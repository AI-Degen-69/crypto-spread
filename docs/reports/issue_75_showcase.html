When trading 5m/15m crypto spread-capture strategies on Polymarket, you often need to inspect the live Polymarket order book or market page. 🏗️ Data Flow & Architecture

How the specific active market slug travels from discovery through execution into the client-side DOM:

1

Market Discovery & Dataclass Foundation

TradeEvent extended with market_slug: str = "" (backward compatible). Engine dynamically tracks m.market_slug alongside m.slug across all 10 series.

2

Unified Slug Propagation

get_open_orders_list() and get_open_positions() propagate both market_slug and series_slug across live CLOB, paper engine, and advance-window states.

3

Client-Side Leg Pairing Preservation

groupOrdersByPair() and groupPositionsByPair() in server/osc_dash.py preserve slugs when merging UP and DOWN legs into unified pair cards.

4

Defensive DOM Hyperlink Rendering

Client JS safely formats https://polymarket.com/market/${encodeURIComponent(mktSlug)} with target="_blank" rel="noopener". If the specific window slug is still discovering, it seamlessly falls back to series_slug.
