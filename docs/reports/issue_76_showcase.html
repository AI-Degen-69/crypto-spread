💡 ELI5: What was the problem and how was it solved?
Imagine you placed two orders on a market (UP and DOWN). A Stop Loss triggered on the UP leg, so the bot cancelled the unhedged DOWN leg. Previously, the dashboard immediately wiped the cancelled DOWN order from the table as if it never existed, and the market card on top continued glowing green and red as if both orders were still active!

Now: The market card immediately shifts to a flat, dimmed stopped state showing inactive bids, and the cancelled DOWN order stays visible in the Orders table with an orange CANCELLED badge until the 5m/15m window finishes and rolls over. No ghost quotes, no disappearing orders.

⚡ Before vs. After

❌ BEFORE (De-synchronized & Ephemeral) Legacy Behavior
  • ⚠️
    Ghost Quoting on Matrix: When a market stopped or timed out, the card still rendered bright green/red active quote pills (`UP $0.48 / DN $0.48`).
  • ⚠️
    Instant Order Disappearance: Cancelled orders were immediately purged from the Orders table, giving zero audit trail of what was cancelled.
  • ⚠️
    Active Button on Dead Orders: Any order marked cancelled still showed an active "Cancel" button, inviting accidental double clicks.
  • ⚠️
    Unchecked Exit Submission: Stop exits attempted opposite cancel and immediate sell quote without verifying cancellation succeeded first.

🔄 End-to-End State Lifecycle

How cancelled orders and matrix cards transition across the trading cycle:

1

Order Cancellation

Stop loss trigger, timeout, or panic cancel cancels opposite unhedged leg. Order is copied into cancelled_orders under _engine_lock.

2

Matrix State Sync

osc_dash.py checks isStoppedOrUnhedgedExit. Card switches from oscillating green/red pills to flat, dimmed inactive bids.

3

Cockpit Table Render

Orders table lists active orders + retained cancelled orders with orange CANCELLED badge and no cancel action button.

4

Window Rollover

Upon window expiry, _handle_window_rollover clears cancelled_orders and resets state cleanly for the next 5m/15m cycle.
