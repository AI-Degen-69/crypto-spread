KNOB 1

Exit Stop Loss 5m / 15m ($)

a.k.a. the panic button 🔔
You hold one ticket at 48¢. The game slides the wrong way. 0.05 = "if the peak drift reaches 5¢ past the 50¢ line, sell it back, eat the small hurt, live to play again."
DRAG ME
wire at 45¢
🪄
Mercy rule (exit_reversal = 0.02) — a verdict, not extra distance: the wire sits 5¢ past fair and the exit fires the moment the peak crosses it — no extra 2¢ gets added. The 2¢ only answers one question: has the price already bounced back within 2¢ of 50¢ since a ≥5¢ dip? If yes, that side's wire is disarmed for the rest of the window (chop ≠ drift) and the robot keeps quoting. Backtest default at backtest/engine.py:121; live unified to the same 0.02 (issue #111).
A · clean slide ✂
Peak crosses 5¢ → sold at the bid that same tick. Mercy never wakes up.
B · flush & bounce 🪄
Crossed 5¢ but no bid to sell into; bounced back within 2¢ of fair → stop disarmed, keep quoting.
C · weak bounce ✂
Bounce stalls at 47.9¢ — outside the 2¢ zone → still stopped out, just at a better price.
⏱️
5m vs 15m: two separate buttons — one for the short games, one for the long ones. Same idea, different patience.

KNOB 4

Partial Windows Filter

a.k.a. skip the half-recorded games 📼
Sometimes the recorder joined the game late. Replaying a half-game is unfair — the robot would have been quoting from the very start. So: drop them?
⚖️
Why it matters: a game that started 8s late is missing the opening — the replay quietly pretends you weren't there for it. Keeping them can flatter (or punish) your scorecard for a bug, not a strategy.
